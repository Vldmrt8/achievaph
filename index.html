ABOUT US

Who We Are

Achieva is a dedicated insurance contracting support team built specifically for Independent Marketing Organizations. We specialize in the operational side of insurance — agent onboarding, contract processing, SureLC management, writing number maintenance, and issue paid reporting — so your team can focus on growth instead of paperwork.

We were built around one simple idea: IMOs shouldn't have to slow down because of administrative bottlenecks. Contracting is detail-heavy, deadline-driven, and easy to fall behind on. Achieva exists to make sure that never happens on your watch. Our team steps in as a seamless extension of your operations — learning your processes, working within your systems, and delivering consistent output you can count on.

Our team brings hands-on experience in IMO workflows and carrier coordination, meaning we understand the work from day one. We don't need a long ramp-up period or constant supervision. We come in ready to handle the details — tracking outstanding requirements, managing SureLC submissions, maintaining writing number records, and compiling monthly reports — so nothing slips through the cracks.

At Achieva, we take pride in being reliable, organized, and easy to work with. We believe that great back-office support should be invisible — you shouldn't have to think about it because it just works. That's the standard we hold ourselves to every day.

SERVICES

What Can We Do for You?

End-to-end contracting support — from agent onboarding to issue paid reports.

Agent Onboarding

We help coordinate the onboarding process by organizing requirements, tracking necessary information, and helping new agents transition smoothly into the contracting process.

Contract Processing

We manage the end-to-end contract submission process — preparing paperwork, submitting to carriers, and tracking status so nothing falls through the cracks.

SureLC Management

We handle SureLC submissions and follow-ups on your behalf, keeping agent contracting requests organized and moving forward without delays.

Outstanding Requirements

We track and follow up on missing or pending requirements so contracts don't stall — keeping your agents and carriers informed at every step.

Writing Number Maintenance

We maintain accurate writing number records across carriers, ensuring agents have the correct identifiers needed to submit business without interruption.

Issue Paid Monthly Reports

We compile and deliver monthly issue paid reports so your team has a clear, up-to-date picture of contracted agents and active production status.
